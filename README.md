# Hi, I'm Shekoofeh 👋

**Senior Full-Stack Engineer · TypeScript · Next.js · NestJS**

I build web products from the interface people use to the systems behind it. Over the past **8+ years**, I've worked across fintech, e-commerce, travel, and enterprise applications.

I enjoy turning complicated requirements into clear, maintainable solutions. That often means working across product decisions, frontend, APIs, data models, and deployment—and thinking about how a feature will work after its first release.

When I'm not building something, you'll probably find me gaming, planning my next trip, or exploring a new idea. 🎮

## ✨ What I Enjoy Building

- **End-to-end product features** — from an idea or design to a working release.
- **Thoughtful frontend experiences** — interfaces that are responsive, accessible, and enjoyable to use.
- **APIs and data flows** — clear contracts, sensible data models, and reliable integrations.
- **Creative web projects** — especially when Three.js or WebGL can make an experience more engaging.
